# Disadvantages of Open Source Software

## What are the disadvantages of Open Source Software?


Open Source Softwares also presents some *less positive* aspects, creating **disadvantages** on several levels:

- **Hidden extra  costs:** Firstly, we have to clarify that *open* doesn't translate into *completely free*. In fact, the software is initially free, but later requires a payment to use it or to access some specific parts, which can be an obslacle when these costs were not considered from the beginning of the project.

- **Lack of official support:** Although the existence of a large community that contributes solving technical issues is an advantage, it also translates into a lack of official support service, and having to rely only on the community or external experts can increase additional costs and longer resolution times of the problem.

- **Uncertain reliability:** Not all open source sources undergo rigorous verification processes, and it's not always possible to confirm the origin of the data or the method used to collect it, which may contain errors or incomplete information. Furthermore, there is a risk that some information may be outdated or not updated regularly.

- **Compatibility issues:** Many proprietary hardware uses specific drivers to run open source softwares, which are often available exclusively from the device manufacturer, making the open source softwares not perform at the same level as proprietary hardware.
