# Advantages of Open Source Software

## What are the advantages of Open Source Software?


 When we talk about Open Source Software, we refer to a *public code* that is available online for everyone, leading to many **advantages**:

 - **Control over the code:** All the useres have full control over the code they want to use and can verify its functioning firsthand, allowing a lot of freedom in their use, without external impositions or limitations.

 - **Freedom of customization:** This control also leads to a great freedom for customization and flexibility for the users, who can modify it to adapt it to their specific needs.

- **Accessibility from a large community:** By reaching a larger community, open source gives anyone the opportunity to learn from an open source project's code, making it suitable also for beginners. Moreover, experienced developers have the opportunity to study the code of open source projects and share their work with others.

- **Financial support**: Although contributions to most open sources are primarily voluntary, some people may get paid to contribute to open source when it is a major project and contributions are ongoing or require significant time.

- **Greater security:** Open source software is also considered more secure than proprietary software, since the code is freely available, so many more developers can identify issues, make updates, and fix bugs more quickly than with proprietary software, where identifying and fixing errors can take longer.
